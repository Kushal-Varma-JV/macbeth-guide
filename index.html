The Story

In a Nutshell

A war hero hears a prophecy that he will be king — and lets his ambition, and his wife, push him toward murder.

Spurred by three witches and by Lady Macbeth, the great general Macbeth kills King Duncan and seizes the throne. To keep his crown he keeps killing — until guilt, paranoia and a gathering rebellion destroy him. It is Shakespeare's shortest, darkest, fastest tragedy.
